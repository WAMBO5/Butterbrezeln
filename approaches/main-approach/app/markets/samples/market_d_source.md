# Market D: vehicle lighting rules (made-up note for the demo, not real law)

Market D applies UN Regulation No. 48 on the installation of lighting and light-signalling devices as a mandatory rule, in its latest version.
Market D accepts approvals to UN Regulation No. 148 on light-signalling devices.
Market D applies UN Regulation No. 10 on electromagnetic compatibility as a mandatory rule.
In addition, every rear lamp sold in Market D must carry the national lamp marking MD-17, a rule outside the UN Regulations.
