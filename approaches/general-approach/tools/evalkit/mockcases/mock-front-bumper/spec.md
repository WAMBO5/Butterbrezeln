# spec: Front Bumper

MOCK SPEC written by the team for a dry run. It is not the organisers' held-out case and has no expected answer.

## Product Type

Front bumper assembly: painted plastic fascia over an energy-absorbing foam layer and a metallic bumper beam, mounted to the front rails of the body-in-white via crash boxes.

## Vehicle Category

M1 — passenger car.

## Function

- Low-speed impact protection of the vehicle front (protective device)
- Pedestrian protection (energy absorption in leg impacts)
- External styling and aerodynamics (air intake grille, lower spoiler)
- Housing for parking-sensor holders and front fog lamp bezels

## Main Parts

- Bumper fascia (cover)
- Energy absorber (foam)
- Bumper beam (reinforcement)
- Crash boxes
- Lower stiffener / spoiler
- Grille
- Mounting brackets
- Parking-sensor holders
- Fog lamp bezels
- Tow-hook cover

## Dimensions

- Bumper height above ground (unladen, front): lower edge approx. 230 mm, upper edge of beam approx. 500 mm
- Overall width: 1,820 mm
- Fascia thickness 3.0 mm; minimum edge radius of exposed outer edges 2.5 mm
